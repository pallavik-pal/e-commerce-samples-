Meet the makers
“Every sari carries a memory. We don't cut it away — we braid it into something that will hold new ones.”

Sunita Devi · Dhaaga Women's Co-operative, Varanasi

Sunita leads a group of 18 women who turn sari offcuts from local tailors into rugs, totes and cushions. Every purchase pays a fair, fixed wage — agreed before the work begins.
